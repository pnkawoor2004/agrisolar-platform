🏛️ Government Capital Support — Policy Simulation Tool

Why direct capital grant is the most effective policy lever: A rupee of capital grant reduces the farmer's net CAPEX by exactly ₹1 — dollar-for-dollar, with no interest burden, no debt service, and no repayment risk. Compare this to an interest subsidy on a term loan: at 4% interest subvention over 7 years, the NPV of the subsidy benefit is only ₹0.55 per rupee of scheme outlay — 45% less efficient. This tool lets policymakers directly read off the IRR, payback, and NPV a farmer achieves at each support level under MIDH (35–50% capital grant, horticulture infrastructure), MoFPI (50–75% grant, cold chain scheme), NABARD RIDF (loan at 5.5%, not a grant — toggle to 0% to model), and PM-KUSUM Component B (30% central + 30% state = 60% combined for solar).

🌿 Lifetime Carbon Impact — Three Scenarios Compared

Full lifecycle analysis over 20 years: operational emissions (avoided or incurred) plus embodied carbon in manufacturing the system components. Grid emission factor: 0.82 kg CO₂/kWh (CEA 2023 national average). Diesel genset: 0.708 kg CO₂/kWh electrical output (2.68 kg CO₂/litre × 0.264 kWh/litre at 37% efficiency). Embodied carbon included for: solar panels (550 kg CO₂/kWp — crystalline Si, IEA 2021), PUF insulation (5.2 kg CO₂/kg × panel mass — ECOINVENT v3), steel mounting structure (1.85 kg CO₂/kg — galvanised steel, ISSF 2023), and refrigeration unit (120 kg CO₂ per TR — manufacturing + refrigerant GWP amortised, ASHRAE).
